# Learn from writing history without imposing a house style

Use only when an authorized history review or durable update includes writing,
editing, translation, scripts, slides, correspondence or document layout. This
reference extracts useful knowledge; it neither writes the requested article nor
activates personal retrieval for ordinary writing. Current drafts and instructions
come first. It does not require another writing Skill, model, language or host.

## Recover the writing task, not a personality label

Retain only evidenced fields needed to resume the work:

- Communicative purpose, audience, central argument and requested reader action.
- User/requester, intended author or narrator, reviewer, and referenced authors.
- Language, genre, channel, factual/fictional framing and disclosure constraints.
- Target draft/section, accepted baseline, requested change and material to retain.
- Delivery form and separate drafted, integrated, reviewed, approved, sent/published
  states, with sources and remaining work.

These are working notes, not a mandatory questionnaire or a fixed article outline.
Missing information is unknown. Ask only when competing interpretations materially
change the result; a terse chat message is not a sample of publishable prose.

## Keep material roles separate

One attachment can contain several roles. Attribute passages as needed:

| Material role | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Brief or direct correction | Purpose, constraints, requested changes | New factual claims about the world |
| Target draft | Existing argument and wording to edit | Truth, ownership, approval or publication |
| Source evidence | Bounded claims at its observation date | Current truth without needed rechecking |
| Voice/structure sample | Rhythm, vocabulary or organization for this task | User identity, copied life events or universal style |
| Background explanation | Understanding the intended concept | A requirement to mention every source in the final copy |
| Reviewer feedback | That reviewer's opinion on a specific version | The user's permanent taste or later-version approval |
| Fictional scenario | Plot, narrator and internal consistency | An actual investment, employment, customer or lived experience |

Retain provenance privately when a public draft omits a source person. Style
adoption never licenses invented non-fiction experience. Explicit fiction is not
silently fact-corrected out of existence; preserve its agreed framing instead.

## Preserve the requested transformation

Differentiate polishing, evidence enrichment, shortening, local replacement,
restructuring, translation, layout-only work and a deliberate change of argument.
"More natural" or "less AI-like" identifies dissatisfaction, not a universal ban
on punctuation, headings or technical terms. Recover the concrete rejected and
preferred forms from context; keep unresolved interpretation uncertain.

Local feedback changes the named section, not the whole article. When a user
explicitly changes the main argument, record that change instead of preserving an
obsolete thesis under a general "keep the original" rule. Facts can remain true
while their rhetorical priority changes. Translation changes language without
silently adding claims; natural phrasing is not permission to change meaning.

For layout-only work, check source-to-output omissions AND output-to-source
additions, including template introductions, author placeholders and calls to
action. Visual emphasis can also change argumentative hierarchy. Record layout
feedback as an artifact correction, not a global dislike of emphasis.

Bind feedback to draft identity, not arrival order. A short "yes" or "good" applies
to the question or version actually reviewed. Saving, requesting translation or
moving on does not establish approval. A finished section is not an integrated
full draft; a full draft is not sent or published.

## Separate three destinations

1. **Artifact/project history:** actual purpose, before/after corrections, sources,
   accepted version and remaining work. This is the normal destination.
2. **Private scoped preference:** only direct or sufficiently supported human
   evidence, subject to [evolution-contract.md](evolution-contract.md). Keep owner,
   genre/language/channel, applicability, counterexamples and later overrides.
3. **Public Skill mechanism:** how to identify scope, provenance and correction,
   with invented examples. No private names, quotations, drafts, paths or style
   fingerprint, even if several local projects repeat it.

Never merge different clients' voices into the user's voice. A rule can be useful
for this artifact without becoming a durable preference. Do not create a personal
writing profile, style-rule library or separate writing Skill merely to optimize
this knowledge pack. Authorization to improve the pack is not consent to export
private writing or impose one person's taste on every installer.

## Check transfer, not just agreement

Use contrasting invented tasks: technical versus conversational prose; different
languages/authors; preserve-thesis versus explicitly replace-thesis; factual versus
fictional work; plain text versus a requested file. A universal rule must allow
both sides when requested. Evaluate observable decisions and outputs, not keywords
or exact preferred phrasing. Report synthetic behavior checks separately from
later real-host performance. Tests may use
`tests/fixtures/writing-history-cases.json` in the development repository.
